Our new paper, "[What Makes Interaction Trajectories Effective for Training Terminal Agents?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03461)", introduces TERMINAL-LEGO and shows that environment-grounded inspect-act-verify trajectories can provide more effective supervision for training terminal agents than stronger teachers' outcome-focused traces.
